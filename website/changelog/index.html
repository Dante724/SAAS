Tekly/What's new

What's new in Tekly.

We ship in small, careful steps and say exactly what's changing. “Coming soon” features are built and in final testing; product screens on this site show sample data.

Coming soon

15 updates in final testing
Coming soon

Missed calls become inquiries

An unanswered call turns into a follow-up with a “Call back” task, so the lead isn't lost.

  • Whoever is clocked in is alerted first
  • Repeat calls from one number join the same inquiry
  • Works with Twilio and Aircall phone systems
Coming soon

Inquiries with a reply-by clock

Note what the customer asked about, which device, and how quickly you need to answer.

  • Inquiry types, each with its own respond-by time
  • Brand, model, serial or IMEI on the inquiry
  • Convert to a job in one tap — the device comes with it
Coming soon

Inquiries in the Tekly Go app

Handle new leads from your phone, with the late ones first.

  • “Late 21 min” or “Reply in 14 min” on every row
  • Call, Start, Mark lost or Convert to job with one thumb
  • New inquiries saved offline and sent once you reconnect
Coming soon

Total profit report

One number for what the shop earned, after tax and commissions.

  • Jobs, dropped jobs and counter sales together
  • Filter by period, location and technician
  • Click through to the jobs and sales behind each figure
Coming soon

Drop a job without phantom debt

A job the customer never collected no longer leaves a balance hanging.

  • Dropping a job asks for a reason
  • The unpaid amount is written off; reopening restores it
  • A separate report shows what each dropped job cost
Coming soon

Linked follow-up jobs

Start the next job for the same customer and device without retyping anything.

  • “Continue as a linked job” from the job menu
  • Customer, device and job fields copied, linked both ways
  • Fault, notes and conclusion can print on the invoice
Coming soon

Scan IMEIs and serials as stock arrives

Today Tekly records the IMEI or serial at intake. Next: every phone or serial-tracked unit is also recorded and checked as stock arrives.

  • One code per unit when receiving a purchase order
  • Duplicates and units already in stock are blocked
  • Warns on a wrong IMEI or a unit you sold before
Coming soon

Paste a purchase order from Excel

Copy rows from a supplier sheet instead of retyping them.

  • Matches by SKU, barcode, MPN or name
  • Creates products that don't exist yet
  • Can mark rows as serialized, IMEI or expiry-tracked
Coming soon

Barcodes for everything

Label every product in a click and scan whatever code is on the box.

  • Generate missing barcodes, SKUs and serials
  • Several barcodes per product
  • Read 12 barcode types, including with the phone camera
Coming soon

One search for plate, IMEI, serial or VIN

Type any identifier, with or without spaces, and find the device or vehicle.

  • Also matches HIN for boats
  • Filter assets: active, written off or all
  • The filter you last used is remembered
Coming soon

See and sign out signed-in devices

Shut off a lost phone or a shared login in a click.

  • Each device with its location and last activity
  • Sign out one device or all the others
  • Optional limit on devices per person
Coming soon

Safer job screen for technicians

A stray click can no longer finish a job.

  • “Save and close” never changes the job status
  • Technician picker with search and avatars
  • Split the work between two technicians, e.g. 50/50
Coming soon

QR labels for every job

Stick a QR code on the device; scanning it opens the job in Tekly Go.

  • “Print QR labels” on the work orders list
  • Tekly Go has a lighter look, with jobs grouped by stage
  • A pilot kit to try the phone app with your team
Coming soon

Tekly Sense follow-ups

Drafts for overdue invoices, unanswered quotes and lapsed customers — never sent without you.

  • Off by default; every draft waits for approval
  • Checks consent and opening hours before sending
  • One-click unsubscribe and an off switch
Coming soon

API v2 and signed webhooks

Connect Tekly to your other tools, safely.

  • Keys limited to what they need, read-only by default
  • Signed webhooks with retries and a delivery log
  • Developer docs for every endpoint

October 2026

9 updates
New

Create jobs, estimates and tasks from the phone

A thumb-reach + button in Tekly Go. Every field is optional, so a job can start with just a name.

  • Customer search with “Add Maria 0412…” inline
  • Device chips, problem chips and photos
  • Tasks grouped by due date, with a badge for today
New

Finish & take payment, right at the bench

The main button does the next step: Mark ready, then Finish & take payment.

  • Part payments, never above what's owed
  • Cash shows the change due
  • Pay-link QR that watches for the balance to reach zero
New

Payment PIN, auto-lock and Face ID

A quick re-check before money on shared or unattended phones.

  • PIN before payments after 5 idle minutes
  • Auto-lock after 10 minutes, or Lock now
  • Optional Face ID / Touch ID per phone
New

Tablet check-in with a drop-off signature

Book a job in on the counter iPad, side by side with a live summary.

  • Your own check-in questions, priority and promised-by date
  • Quote lines, deposit and condition photos
  • Print the ticket or text the tracking link
New

Technician tools on every job

Start, pause or switch the job timer, and a queue that puts overdue work first.

  • Bench notes and a conclusion the customer sees
  • Photos labelled Check-in, Before, After, Damage
  • “Part not here?” raises an urgent task for the manager
New

Credit notes and partial payments

Billing that handles real life: record part of a payment, and decide what happens to an overpayment.

  • Numbered credit notes: draft → issued → cancelled
  • Overpaid? Keep it as credit or refund it
  • Choose how invoices display
New

Chats that tell you who's waiting

Every conversation is New, Reply needed, Replied or Completed, and finishes itself after 24 hours.

  • A spam folder for the noise
  • Message variables: first name, portal link, balance due
  • Totals always in your shop's currency
New

My earnings, for every technician

Each person sees what they've earned. Owners get payroll filters, drill-down and scope (own, all or selected).

  • Checklists attach themselves on status change
  • MPN on products, and pick which field becomes the label barcode
  • The web app now fits phones and iPads
Rolling out

Card readers, scan-to-cart and calling

Scan straight into the POS cart. Stripe Terminal and Square Terminal readers, and calls through Twilio with contacts created from calls and click-to-call.

  • Live once your shop connects its own accounts
