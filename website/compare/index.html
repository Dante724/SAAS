Tekly vs RepairDesk

RepairDesk describes itself as cloud-based point-of-sale software for small and medium-sized repair shops, and lists cell phone, computer, small engine, power tool, jewellery, watch, bicycle, drone, camera, heavy-duty and mail-in repair.

RepairDesk: From $79/mo billed annually or $99/mo billed quarterly (Essential, 5 users, per store)
Tekly: $29/mo (Solo, up to 2 people), billed monthly

Tekly vs RepairShopr

RepairShopr lists computer, cell phone, electronics, IT/MSP, general repair, watch, scuba, musical instrument, small engine and lawn care shops among the businesses it serves.

RepairShopr: $69.99/mo monthly or $59.99/mo annual (Starter: 1 user, 1 location, 75 tickets and invoices a month)
Tekly: $29/mo (Solo, up to 2 people, 150 jobs a month)

Tekly vs Shopmonkey

Shopmonkey lists auto repair, tire, quick lube, heavy-duty and wrap-and-detail shops, and has a page for boat repair shops.

Shopmonkey: $239/mo monthly or $215/mo annual (Basic, 3 user licenses)
Tekly: $29/mo (Solo, 2 people); $69/mo (Shop, 8 people)

Tekly vs Tekmetric

Tekmetric serves auto repair and tire shops and says more than 16,000 shops use it.

Tekmetric: $199/mo monthly or $179/mo annual (Start), per shop
Tekly: $29/mo (Solo); $69/mo (Shop, 8 people); $129/mo (Pro)

Tekly vs Orderry

Orderry describes itself as an all-in-one platform for small businesses, covering electronics and phone repair, auto and truck, small engines and power tools, marine, bicycle, jewellery, HVAC and contractors, field services and retail.

Orderry: $39/mo (Hobby: 2 employees, 1 location, 100 work orders or sales per 30 days)
Tekly: $29/mo (Solo, 2 people, 150 jobs a month)
