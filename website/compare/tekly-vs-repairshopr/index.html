RepairShopr may suit you better if…

  • You run an IT or managed-service business as well as a repair bench — RepairShopr lists IT/MSP work.

Tekly may suit you better if…

  • You're a small shop: Tekly's Solo plan is $29 a month for 2 people, and Shop is $69 for 8.

What is a good alternative to RepairShopr?

It depends on your trade and team. Tekly is an alternative to RepairShopr for phone, computer and electronics repair shops: published prices from $29 a month, a 14-day free trial with no card, and free data migration. The comparison above lists where RepairShopr may suit you better.

Can I switch from RepairShopr to Tekly?

Yes. Send an export from RepairShopr or your spreadsheets and Tekly brings customers, devices or vehicles and stock across, free on every plan. Most single-location shops are set up in a day.
