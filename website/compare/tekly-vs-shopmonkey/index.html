Tekly vs Shopmonkey

Shopmonkey is auto repair shop software with estimates, digital authorizations, payments and parts ordering. Tekly covers auto repair alongside electronics, small engine and marine, at a lower published price, with payroll on Pro.

Shopmonkey may suit you better if…

  • You want parts lookup and ordering built in — Shopmonkey lists it on every plan.
  • You run a tire, quick-lube or wrap-and-detail shop, which Shopmonkey targets directly.
  • You want buy-now-pay-later and Apple Pay / Google Pay listed on every plan.

Tekly may suit you better if…

  • Price matters: Tekly's Shop plan is $69 a month for 8 people, against Shopmonkey's published $239 for 3.
  • You want a free trial before talking to sales, and free data migration.
  • Declined work should be saved and brought back at the customer's next visit.
  • You also fix bikes, small engines or electronics in the same business, with intake fields for each (IMEI, engine hours, HIN).

Going by published prices as of 23 October 2026: Tekly starts at $29 a month (Solo, up to 2 people), or $69 for 8 people on Shop. Shopmonkey's published starting price is $239/mo monthly or $215/mo annual (Basic, 3 user licenses). Compare what your whole team would pay, including extra people and locations.

What is a good alternative to Shopmonkey?

It depends on your trade and team. Tekly is an alternative to Shopmonkey for auto repair shops: published prices from $29 a month, a 14-day free trial with no card, and free data migration. The comparison above lists where Shopmonkey may suit you better.

Can I switch from Shopmonkey to Tekly?

Yes. Send an export from Shopmonkey or your spreadsheets and Tekly brings customers, devices or vehicles and stock across, free on every plan. You can take your first job the day you sign up.
