Tekly vs Orderry

Orderry and Tekly are both all-in-one systems for repair businesses with estimates, inventory, payroll and AI features. Orderry also covers field service and HVAC; Tekly focuses on bench repair with more people included in the price.

Looking for a Orderry alternative?

Tekly and Orderry, by the published facts.

Orderry describes itself as an all-in-one platform for small businesses, covering electronics and phone repair, auto and truck, small engines and power tools, marine, bicycle, jewellery, HVAC and contractors, field services and retail. Orderry may suit you better if…

  • You run field service, HVAC or contracting work — Orderry lists these and Tekly doesn't.
  • You need a very large team on one plan (Orderry's Enterprise plan goes up to 1,500 employees).
  • You want payroll and commissions on every plan — Orderry lists them on all plans; Tekly has payroll on Pro.

Tekly may suit you better if…

  • You want more people included: Shop has 8 people for $69 a month, against 3 on Orderry's Startup.
  • You'd like a longer trial: 14 days of every Pro feature, against Orderry's 7.
  • Customers should approve or decline each line of an estimate on their phone and sign, including extra work found mid-repair.
  • Tekly AI drafts the estimate from the customer's words on Pro, from your own catalog and prices.

Is Tekly cheaper than Orderry?

Going by published prices as of 23 October 2026: Tekly starts at $29 a month (Solo, up to 2 people), or $69 for 8 people on Shop. Orderry's published starting price is $39/mo (Hobby: 2 employees, 1 location, 100 work orders or sales per 30 days). Compare what your whole team would pay, including extra people and locations.

What is a good alternative to Orderry?

It depends on your trade and team. Tekly is an alternative to Orderry for electronics, vehicle, small engine and marine repair shops: published prices from $29 a month, a 14-day free trial with no card, and free data migration. The comparison above lists where Orderry may suit you better.

Can I switch from Orderry to Tekly?

Yes. Send an export from Orderry or your spreadsheets and Tekly brings customers, devices or vehicles and stock across, free on every plan. Most single-location shops are set up in a day.
