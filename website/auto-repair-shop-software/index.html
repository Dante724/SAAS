How a job runs

From check-in to paid, in five steps.

  1. 01

    Check in

    Find the vehicle by VIN or plate, log the mileage and take condition photos at the counter. Every visit stays on the vehicle's history.

  2. 02

    Estimate

    On Pro, Tekly AI turns “grinding when braking, pulls left” into an inspection, pads, rotors and labour at your prices. You review and send.

  3. 03

    Approval

    The customer approves each line on their phone and signs. Declined work is saved for the next visit.

  4. 04

    Parts

    Order from the repair order. Received parts are charged to the right vehicle and their cost lands in the job's margin.

  5. 05

    Pickup and pay

    Payment at the counter or by link. Technician commission and hours post when the job closes, ready for the pay run.

Questions

Vehicle repair, answered.

More questions? Read the full FAQ

Does Tekly work for truck, fleet and RV repair?

Yes. The vehicle template covers cars, trucks, RVs and bikes, with unit numbers and account billing for fleets.

Can customers approve repairs by text?

Yes. They get a link by SMS or WhatsApp, approve each line and sign on their phone.

Does it keep each vehicle's service history?

Every job stays on the vehicle's record, found by VIN or plate.

How much does auto repair shop software cost with Tekly?

Plans start at $29 a month for up to 2 people. Shop is $69 a month for 4 people across your locations and adds inventory, scanning, the time clock and reports; Pro is $129 and adds purchase orders, payroll and commissions, AI estimate drafting and QuickBooks or Xero sync. Prices are published and month to month.

Can I switch from Shopmonkey or another shop system?

Yes. Send an export from your current system or spreadsheets and we bring customers, vehicles and stock across, free on every plan.

Does Tekly work with QuickBooks?

Yes. Pro syncs invoices and payments with QuickBooks or Xero both ways.
