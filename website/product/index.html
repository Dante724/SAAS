Tekly Go

The whole bench, in every technician's pocket.

Tekly Go comes with every plan. Install it to the home screen straight from the browser — no app store. Technicians see today's jobs, run the labour timer, tick off checklists, scan parts and clock in, even when the Wi-Fi drops.

  • Today's jobs with customer, device and complaint
  • Labour timer and step-by-step checklists on every job
  • Scan parts, assets and QR labels with the camera
  • Time clock with breaks, approved on the desktop
  • Works offline and syncs when the connection is back

Tekly Sense

Suggestions that come from your own closed jobs.

Tekly Sense reads the jobs you've already closed and turns the patterns into one-tap suggestions. Each one has a “Why?” that shows the jobs behind it. It runs on Tekly's own servers, and nothing is shared between shops.
