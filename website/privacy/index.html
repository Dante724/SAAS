Tekly/Privacy policy

Privacy policy

Last updated 19 October 2026

This policy explains what Tekly collects on this website and in the Tekly app, why, who processes it for us, and the choices you have. Tekly is operated by [[COMPANY LEGAL NAME]], [[REGISTERED ADDRESS]] ("Tekly", "we"). Questions: privacy@tekly.app.

Two roles

For our own visitors and account holders (shop owners and their staff) we decide what is collected and why: we are the controller.

For the data a shop keeps in Tekly about its own customers (names, phone numbers, devices, jobs, photos, signatures, payments) the shop is the controller and we process it only on the shop's instructions, under our terms. If you are a shop's customer, contact that shop first; we will help them answer you.

On this website

  • Demo requests and the on-page assistant. When you ask for a demo we receive your name, work email, phone number, shop name and any message. The assistant runs in your browser; nothing you type in it is sent anywhere unless you send a demo request or book a time.
  • Where you came from. We keep the campaign tags in the address you arrived on (for example utm_source) and the referring site in your browser's session storage for that visit, and attach them to a demo request so we know which channel worked. They are cleared when you close the tab.
  • No cookies, no third-party trackers. This site sets no cookies and loads no analytics or advertising scripts. Fonts are served from our own server. If we ever add analytics, we will ask for your consent first and update this page.

In the Tekly app

  • Account data: name, email, password (stored only as a one-way hash), role, sign-in activity, and two-step verification settings.
  • Shop data you add: customers and their contact details, devices and identifiers (IMEI, serial, VIN, HIN), work orders, notes, photos and files, signatures, estimates, invoices, payments, inventory, suppliers, appointments and messages.
  • Staff data: time-clock entries (with location only if the staff member allows it on their device), timesheets, wages, commissions and payroll.
  • Technical data: IP address and browser details in security logs, and error reports with personal data removed.

Why we use it

  • To provide the service a shop signed up for (the contract with the shop).
  • To keep accounts secure, prevent abuse and fix errors (our legitimate interest in a safe, working service).
  • To answer demo requests and support questions, and to send service emails such as verification, password reset and invitations.
  • To meet legal duties such as tax and accounting records.

We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.

Who processes it for us

We use these providers only for the purpose listed. Some are used only when a shop turns the feature on.

  • Hosting and database: [[HOSTING PROVIDER AND REGION]].
  • File storage (photos, documents): Amazon Web Services S3, when enabled.
  • Email delivery: [[EMAIL PROVIDER]].
  • SMS and WhatsApp: Twilio; Messenger, Instagram and WhatsApp Cloud: Meta. Used when a shop connects them.
  • Card payments: Stripe, Square or SumUp, through the shop's own account with that provider. Card details go straight to the provider; Tekly never sees them.
  • AI features: Anthropic. When staff use an AI feature (job summaries, estimate drafts, reply suggestions), the relevant text is sent to Anthropic to produce the result. Under Anthropic's commercial terms this data is not used to train their models.
  • Accounting and mailbox connections: QuickBooks Online, Xero, Gmail and Microsoft Outlook, when a shop connects them.
  • Error tracking: Sentry, with request bodies, cookies and sign-in headers removed.

Some of these providers are outside your country. Where the law requires it, transfers are covered by standard contractual clauses or an equivalent safeguard.

How long we keep it

Shop data is kept while the shop's account is open. When a shop closes its account, we delete or anonymise its data within [[NUMBER]] days, except records we must keep by law (such as invoices). Demo requests are kept for up to 24 months. Security logs are kept for up to 12 months.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Passwords are hashed, integration credentials are encrypted at rest, and accounts can use two-step verification. Each shop's data is kept separate from every other shop's, and staff see only what their role allows.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to see, correct, export or delete your personal data, object to or restrict how we use it, and complain to your data protection authority. Shops can export and erase a customer's data from inside Tekly. To make a request about your own account or a demo request, email privacy@tekly.app; we answer within 30 days.

Children

Tekly is a business tool and is not meant for anyone under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and tell account holders by email before the change takes effect.
