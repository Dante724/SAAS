# Tekly marketing site

Static site — no build step. Preview locally:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 4321 --directory website
```

## What to edit where

| Change | File |
|---|---|
| Plans (Solo $29, Shop $69 + $49/location + $4/extra person, Pro $129 + $79/location), features, yearly months, trial | `site.config.js` → `plans`, `yearlyMonths`, `trialDays` — keep in step with `backend/services/billing.py` |
| App sign-in / sign-up URLs | `site.config.js` → `appUrl`, `signInPath`, `signUpPath` |
| Where demo requests go | `site.config.js` → `demoEndpoint` (POST JSON) or `demoEmail` (mailto fallback) |
| Copy and sections | `index.html` |

## Design system
Follows `algolia.design.md`: a near-black hero washed with a violet-to-cobalt gradient and a
stacked three-word Sora headline ("Diagnose. Approve. Get paid."), then a white editorial body
with hairline (#e5e7eb) cards at 12px corners, and a deep-navy (#000033) product band ending in
large Sora 900 proof numerals (honest product facts, no invented stats). Two typefaces only:
Sora for display, headings and numbers; Inter for everything you read. Electric blue #003dff is
held for pill CTAs and inline links; pills are used for buttons only.

No animation libraries: reveal-on-scroll and the demos are plain JS with IntersectionObserver.
Interactive pieces: the complaint → AI estimate card in the hero, the five job-stage tabs in the
navy band, the phone approval demo, the pricing toggle and the demo / sign-up / sign-in panels.

Measured (headless Chrome): scroll 60 fps, no horizontal overflow from 320px to 1440px;
Lighthouse performance 94 mobile / 98 desktop, accessibility, best practices and SEO 100.

## Visuals
No photography. Every visual is a product screen built in HTML/CSS with sample data (labelled as
such): the AI estimate card, the five job-stage previews, the phone approval demo, a purchase
order, a pay run with revenue chart, and the customer's tracking page. Trades are shown as the
four real templates — Electronics (IMEI or serial, with a Phone/Computer toggle), Vehicles
(cars, trucks, RVs, bikes), Tools & small engine, and Marine — with line icons and example intake.

Trust content is factual: a "works with" strip lists integrations that exist in the codebase
(Stripe, Square, QuickBooks, Xero, SMS via Twilio, WhatsApp, Gmail, Outlook) and a promises row
(no contracts, export anytime, free migration, AI never sends on its own).

## SEO & marketing

- **Pages:** homepage plus four landing pages, one per template, each targeting how shops search:
  `/phone-repair-software/`, `/auto-repair-shop-software/`, `/small-engine-repair-software/`,
  `/marine-repair-software/`. They're generated by `python3 website/tools/build_pages.py`, which
  also writes `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt` and `site.webmanifest`. Edit copy in that script and rerun.
- **On every page:** keyword title (< 60 chars), meta description (< 155), canonical URL, Open
  Graph + Twitter card (`assets/og.png`, 1200×630), favicon/app icons, one H1 with the main keyword.
- **Structured data (JSON-LD):** Organization, WebSite, SoftwareApplication with all three plan prices,
  and FAQPage on the homepage (generated from the visible FAQ); WebPage, BreadcrumbList and
  FAQPage on each landing page. No ratings or reviews are claimed.
- **Attribution:** `marketing.js` keeps `utm_*`, `gclid`, `fbclid`, `msclkid` and `ref` for the
  visit and appends them to every sign-up / sign-in link and to demo-form submissions.
- **Analytics:** CTA clicks (`cta_click`) and demo requests (`generate_lead`) are pushed to
  `window.dataLayer`. Set `analytics.gtmId` in `site.config.js` to load Google Tag Manager; nothing
  third-party loads until you do (add a cookie banner first if you serve EU/UK visitors).

**Switching the domain** (currently `https://tekly.app`) everywhere, from the repo root:

```bash
grep -rl "https://tekly.app" website | xargs sed -i '' 's#https://tekly.app#https://YOUR-DOMAIN#g'
```

Then submit `https://YOUR-DOMAIN/sitemap.xml` in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.

## Tekly Assistant (on-page chat)

`assistant.js` adds an "Ask Tekly" chat to every page. It runs entirely in the browser — no AI
service is called and no API key is needed. It answers from a built-in knowledge base of facts
that appear on the site (plans, templates, AI features, integrations, switching, data), with
typo-tolerant matching; recommends Solo, Shop or Pro from a few questions (9+ people without Pro needs → Shop plus $4 seats, same rule as the app); and books demos.
Off-topic questions get an honest "I'm not sure" and an offer to book a person.

**Booking real times:** create a "Tekly Demos" shop in Tekly, turn on online appointments and
set its working hours, then put its booking slug in `site.config.js → assistant.booking.slug`
(and `timezoneLabel`, e.g. "ET"). The assistant then shows live open days and times from
Tekly's own booking API and books straight into that calendar (double-booking is handled by
the API). Add the website's domain to the backend's `CORS_ORIGINS`.
Without a slug, it collects a preferred day and morning/afternoon and sends a demo request
(to `demoEndpoint`, or the visitor's email app).

Events pushed to `dataLayer`: `assistant_open`, `assistant_message` (matched intent),
`assistant_booking_start`, `assistant_recommend`, `generate_lead`. To edit answers, change the
`KB` and `TRADES` lists at the top of `assistant.js` — keep them to facts that are true.

## Homepage v2 preview (`/v2/`)

`v2/index.html` is a redesigned homepage, kept next to the live one until it's approved: one
self-contained file (inline CSS and an ES module; the only library is Lenis 1.1.18, self-hosted at
`assets/vendor/`) with the same copy, plans (`site.config.js`), links, demo destination, FAQ,
JSON-LD, attribution and assistant. It's `noindex` with a canonical to `/` while it's a preview.

- **Layout:** rounded "card" sections on a light page; a full-height photo hero with a navy scrim,
  a navy stats band and a navy CTA footer. Geist and Tekly sapphire.
- **Speed:** the loader shows once per visit for at most 1.2s; the hero photo is a 65KB 1600w
  WebP (960/1600/2400 srcset, plus a portrait crop for phones); reveals run on the compositor
  (Web Animations), scroll work is one rAF per frame with geometry measured on resize; the
  assistant loads when the browser is idle.
- **Motion:** a small rAF spring helper drives hovers (off at ≤768px), the templates carousel, the
  menu and the demo modal. `prefers-reduced-motion` skips Lenis and shows everything at once.
- **SEO:** one H1 with the keyword visible; job stages and the four template cards (with their
  landing-page links) are in the HTML, not injected by JS. Pricing cards are still rendered from
  `site.config.js` (prices are also in the FAQ and JSON-LD).
- **Every trade, every location:** the four template cards (with live photo tiles that cycle
  2–3 photos while on screen) sit side by side on desktop right after the intro, followed by a
  "One account for every repair business you run" band (trades, locations, stock transfers,
  per-location numbers). Below 1025px they become a swipeable carousel.
- **New sections:** What's new (from the Tekly Go / parity PRs; card readers and calling are
  marked "Rolling out" until live keys are set) and About us.
- **Placeholders to fill before launch:** the founder note in About us; the customer-quotes
  section is `hidden` until real quotes exist (`PLACEHOLDER` comments in the HTML).
- **Photos:** `assets/photos/` from Unsplash (Unsplash License) and Wikimedia Commons (public
  domain, CC BY 2.0, CC BY-SA 3.0/4.0). Credits are listed under "Photo credits" in the footer;
  keep them when you swap photos.

To make it the homepage: move `v2/index.html` over `index.html`, change `robots` back to
`index, follow, max-image-preview:large`, and update `lastmod` for `/` in `sitemap.xml`. Keep
`styles.css` (the landing pages still use it).
