About us

We build Tekly for the people at the bench.

Repair is personal work. Someone hands you their phone, their truck or their boat and trusts you with it. We think the software behind your counter should earn that same trust — so it's built around a few promises we don't bend on.

We're a small team, and when you write to us, a person writes back. Tell us what slows your shop down and it goes straight onto our list. Most of what's new on this page started as a shop's request.
