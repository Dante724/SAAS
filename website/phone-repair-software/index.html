How a job runs

From check-in to paid, in five steps.

  1. 01

    Check in

    Pick Phone, Tablet, Laptop or Desktop. Tekly asks for the IMEI or serial, stores the passcode encrypted and takes condition photos at the counter.

  2. 02

    Estimate

    On Pro, Tekly AI turns “dropped in the pool, won't charge” into priced lines from your own catalog. You review and send.

  3. 03

    Approval

    The customer approves each line on their phone and signs. A cracked back glass found mid-repair waits for their tick.

  4. 04

    Parts

    Order the screen from the ticket. Scan the box when it arrives and it's charged to that repair, with the cost in the margin.

  5. 05

    Pickup and pay

    The tracking link says it's ready. They pay at the counter or online, and the technician's commission posts when the job closes.

Questions

Phone and computer repair, answered.

More questions? Read the full FAQ

Does Tekly record IMEI numbers?

Yes. The electronics template asks for the IMEI on phones and tablets and the serial number on computers, and keeps it on the device's history.

Are customer passcodes stored safely?

Passcodes are encrypted at rest and only visible to staff whose role allows it.

Can I sell accessories and refurbished devices?

Yes. The built-in POS handles retail sales alongside repairs, with stock tracked by location and serial number.

How much does phone repair software cost?

Tekly starts at $29 a month for up to 2 people. Shop is $69 a month for 8 people and adds inventory and barcode scanning; Pro is $129 and adds purchase orders, payroll and AI estimate drafting. Every trial includes all Pro features for 14 days with no card.

Can I move my customers and devices from my current system?

Yes. Send an export from your current system or spreadsheets and we bring customers, devices and stock across, free on every plan. Most single-location shops are set up in a day.

Do customers get updates without calling the shop?

Yes. Each repair has a tracking link with status, photos and the ready time, and updates go out by SMS, WhatsApp or email.
