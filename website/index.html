    Questions

    Straight answers.

    Something else? Ask a person

    How long does it take to switch?

    Most single-location shops can be set up in a day. Send us an export from your current system or spreadsheets and we bring customers, devices and stock across as part of onboarding.

    What's the difference between Starter and Pro?

    Starter covers the counter and the bench for a single shop with up to five people. Pro adds purchase orders, payroll and commissions, multi-location stock, the full set of AI tools, accounting sync, SSO and priority support.

    Will AI ever message my customers by itself?

    No. Tekly AI drafts estimates, replies and summaries, and a person on your team decides what gets sent.

    Do I need special hardware?

    No. Tekly runs in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone, and works with standard barcode scanners, label printers and receipt printers.

    What happens to my data if I leave?

    It's yours. Export everything to CSV or Excel at any time, including after you cancel.
