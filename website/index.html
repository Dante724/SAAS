13Questions

Straight answers.

Something else? Ask a person

How long does it take to switch?

Most single-location shops can be set up in a day. Send us an export from your current system or spreadsheets and our team brings customers, devices and stock across as part of onboarding.

Is Tekly AI really included?

Yes. Every plan gets job summaries and reply suggestions. Pro adds estimate drafting and parts-list recognition; Scale adds call transcripts with sentiment. There is no per-message AI fee.

Will AI ever message my customers by itself?

No. Tekly AI drafts — estimates, replies, summaries — and a person on your team decides what gets sent.

Do I need special hardware?

No. Tekly runs in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone, and works with standard barcode scanners, label printers and receipt printers.

Can I run more than one kind of repair?

Yes. A shop that fixes phones and e-bikes, or cars and boats, can use several templates side by side — each job gets the right fields.

What happens to my data if I leave?

It's yours. Export everything to CSV or Excel at any time, including after you cancel.
