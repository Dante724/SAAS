# Pre-launch QA — 19 Oct 2026

What was tested: `security/phase-4` (b4097a6) merged with the phone-app PRs #8–#10 (`go/pay-reauth`),
running locally on a fresh demo shop (SQLite). Three screen sizes (desktop 1440, iPad 820, phone 390),
three users (owner, technician, logged-out customer).

- **Automated sweep:** 212 page loads plus every tab.
- **Click-everything pass:** about 500 buttons and rows clicked, like a curious new user.
- **Speed run:** an idle server.
- **Hands-on walkthroughs of the main flows:**
  - sign in;
  - new order → add items → status → finish → payment;
  - customer tracking page and portal;
  - booking;
  - POS sale;
  - phone app: new job → add item → status → scan job number and IMEI;
  - PIN screen.

## What is solid

- No JavaScript crashes, broken internal links, broken images or "undefined"/"NaN" text anywhere in
  the sweep. The only external links are `tel:` links.
- **Speed:** every page settles in under 150 ms and every API call takes under 80 ms (local, small
  shop). There are no main-thread stalls and nothing scrolls sideways.
- **Flows that worked end to end:**
  - sign-in;
  - adding items to an order;
  - status changes;
  - cash payment marks the invoice Paid;
  - tracking page;
  - portal;
  - POS sale (stock goes down);
  - phone app: new job, add item, change status;
  - scan by job number and by IMEI;
  - "link expired" pages.
- **Combined code:** 1670 backend tests pass. The only failures come from merging the branches
  (see B4). Frontend `tsc`, UI-text lint, build and 244 vitest tests pass.

## Polish (smoothness, no breakage)

- **Home:** the dashboard jumps while loading (layout shift 0.15–0.21; the target is under 0.1), and
  again when changing location. The "In your accounts" tile clips "₹130,000".
- **New-order form:** it asks "Fault reported" and "Complaint / description", which is the same
  thing twice. The item picker shows prices without ₹ and the jargon badges "svc"/"prod"/"kit".
  Picking an item adds it straight away there, but in the order drawer you also have to press "Add".
- **Order list at 1024 px:** "WO-00001" wraps onto two lines and the Action column is cut off.
- **Payment popup:** its dark overlay leaves a white strip at the top of the screen.
- **Paid job:** it still offers "Take Payment" and "Requested deposit". "Settled" uses the same yellow
  badge as "Balance due". "Mark done and close" still shows on a done job.
- **Phone app:**
  - the "+" button covers the first card's status badge;
  - the sticky "Create job"/"Mark …" buttons cover form content;
  - the add-item sheet doesn't focus its search box;
  - long names are cut off;
  - it shows "₹0.00" where the web app shows "₹0";
  - the Timer message says "use switch" but there is no switch button;
  - the PIN sheet reads "Set up your PIN: set a PIN first."
- **Tap targets under 24 px** on iPad/phone in Catalog, Contacts and Inventory. Unlabeled icon-only
  buttons on Tasks and in the order drawer, so screen readers announce nothing.
- **Settings:** the checklist says "Add your shop details" isn't done although the shop has a name
  and address. There are two settings search boxes on one page.
- **Flaky tests:** `CashPanel.test.tsx` and `CustomView.test.tsx` fail when the machine is busy and pass
  when it's idle, so they can flake CI.
